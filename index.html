01 Statement

Two ways of seeing the same thing.

A cǎi-biān-bō degree teaches you where a sentence should land — the breath before it, the weight at the end. It also teaches you that the sentence is only one part of the job: someone still has to find the story, hold the camera, and cut it until it holds.

I am applying to Communication (AI and Digital Media) because I want to work where those two habits meet: images that carry an argument, and tools that let an idea be tested before it exists.

Everything on this site was made in the last two years — some in a darkroom, some in a latent space. I keep both, because I don't think one replaces the other.

A programme that doesn't exist yet

Life, Restart

A pitch for what may be China's first AI-assisted interactive drama: a real actor lives a life, the audience votes at every fork, and the model generates the branch that wins.

  1. 01

    The gap

    Short drama is a huge market with one limit — you can only watch. Generated video is what makes interactive branches affordable.

  2. 02

    The shape

    Five beats in five minutes: recap, advance, a real choice, the host's analysis, the vote. Twelve choice points make one season.

  3. 03

    The host

    A model can generate the branch. It cannot hold a room. The host reads the vote, argues both sides and owns the recommendation.

    05 About

    One person, whole crew

    Zhengzhou, 2026

    I read Broadcasting and Hosting at Henan University of Technology, but the degree is built on cǎi-biān-bō — reporting, editing and hosting trained as one job rather than three. So the four years were never elocution lessons: television camera work, non-linear editing, news writing, audio and Python sat beside the voice training, and every assignment had to be researched, shot, cut and finally presented by the same person.

    That is where the range comes from. I can shoot a documentary feature about the alleys at the foot of the Shang city wall, cut a horror short about a corridor that keeps returning you to the same door, and host the campus newscast — often in the same month, sometimes in the same week.

    Generative tools did not make that range redundant. They made it faster to test an idea, and harder to tell which idea is worth testing — which is exactly the judgement I want a Communication degree to sharpen: how images persuade, who gets to speak, and what a host is for once a host can be synthetic.
