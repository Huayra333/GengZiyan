Life, Restart

A pitch for what may be China's first AI-assisted interactive drama: a real actor lives a life, the audience votes at every fork, the model generates the branch that wins — and the document around it works out the market, the budget and the risk.

  1. 01

    The gap

    Short drama is a huge market with one limit — you can only watch. Generated video is what makes interactive branches affordable.

  2. 02

    The shape

    Five beats in five minutes: recap, advance, a real choice, the host's analysis, the vote. Twelve choice points make one season.

  3. 03

    The host

    A model can generate the branch. It cannot hold a room. The host reads the vote, argues both sides and owns the recommendation.

    04 About

    One person, whole crew

    Zhengzhou, 2026

    I read Broadcasting and Hosting at Henan University of Technology, but the degree is built on cǎi-biān-bō — reporting, editing and hosting trained as one job rather than three. So the four years were never elocution lessons: television camera work, non-linear editing, news writing, audio and Python sat beside the voice training, and every assignment had to be researched, shot, cut and finally presented by the same person.

    That is where the range comes from. I can shoot a documentary feature about the alleys at the foot of the Shang city wall, cut a horror short about a corridor that keeps returning you to the same door, host the campus newscast — and write the proposal that would put a format like that on a platform: market, competitors, budget, host, business model, risk. Often in the same month, sometimes in the same week.

    Generative tools did not make that range redundant. They made it faster to test an idea and harder to tell which idea is worth testing, and they changed what a production costs — which changes who can afford to make one at all. That is the judgement I want two years to sharpen: how images persuade and who gets to speak; and how a good idea survives a schedule, a budget and an audience it has not won yet.
