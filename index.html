01 Statement

Two ways of seeing the same thing.

A camera taught me to wait. A diffusion model taught me to keep asking. Both are ways of paying attention — one to light, the other to language.

I am applying to Communication (AI and Digital Media) because I want to work where those two habits meet: images that carry an argument, and tools that let an idea be tested before it exists.

Everything on this site was made in the last two years — some in a darkroom, some in a latent space. I keep both, because I don't think one replaces the other.

PROPOSAL / 2026

A programme that doesn't exist yet

Life, Restart

A pitch for what may be China's first AI-assisted interactive drama: a real actor lives a life, the audience votes at every fork, and the model generates the branch that wins.

  1. 01

    The gap

    Short drama is a huge market with one limit — you can only watch. Generated video is what makes interactive branches affordable.

  2. 02

    The shape

    Five beats in five minutes: recap, advance, a real choice, the host's analysis, the vote. Twelve choice points make one season.

  3. 03

    The host

    A model can generate the branch. It cannot hold a room. The host reads the vote, argues both sides and owns the recommendation.

    05 About

    Somebody behind the frames

    Zhengzhou, 2026

    I'm a student who got into photography through the back door — first editing other people's footage, then learning to shoot so I'd have something of my own to cut.

    When generative tools appeared, they felt like the same problem in a different costume: framing, timing, and knowing when to stop. I've spent the last two years testing that instinct — building prompts the way I build a shot list, and rejecting ninety percent of the output.

    What I want from a Communication degree is the missing half: the theory of how images persuade, and the company of people who argue about it.
